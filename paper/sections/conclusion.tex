\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

The main experimental result of this paper is the neuron-usage gap in
\S\ref{sec:exp-analysis}: at matched spike counts, active neurons dropped by
13.1\% under the proposed regulariser relative to Plain $L_2$, despite both
landing on the same accuracy-spike curve.
% GATE: G4
Regularisation strength was set through one ratio tracked against the task
loss during training, rather than a pre-set spike target or a hand-tuned
coefficient -- a tentative distinction from prior work, pending a control
that isolates the ratio controller from the choice of per-neuron weighting.
Hardware validation on Speck and extension to the 173M-parameter SDT-V3
architecture remain for future work.
